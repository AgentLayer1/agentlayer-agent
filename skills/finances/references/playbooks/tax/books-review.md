# Books review

**You own that the operator approves the accountant's books knowing what is in them, what it costs, and what to ask.** The accountant sent management accounts, a general ledger, or a trial balance for a period, and the operator wants them read before saying yes; or "review the books", "what's in the accountant's accounts", "is anything wrong in the books?".

1. **Gather the source:** every workbook or PDF the accountant sent for the period, read where it lives (the profile's `documents` folder, or a path the operator gives). Note each file in the review's `sources`. When periods overlap or a later file rolls earlier profit into retained earnings, say which file each figure comes from, so an opening balance never reads as the year's result.
2. **The year on one page:** sales, cost of sales, the largest expense lines, profit before tax, and tax provided, each with the accountant's figure and the file it came from.
3. **Balance sheet at the period end:** cash by account (one line per account in the profile's `## Accounts`; an account in the profile with no line in the books is a finding), receivables, amounts due from or to the director, deposits, fixed assets, accruals, equity.
4. **The director balance, when there is one:** split it into buckets from the ledger lines (transfers to the operator, card spend with no receipt, personal costs paid by the company, repayments, offsets, the opening balance), with each bucket's total and largest items.
5. **Findings, ranked by what they cost.** Look for: personal costs in company books, card or payment-platform spend with no receipt, withholding or imported-service tax computed but not paid or not accrued, prepayments booked as expenses, an expense booked to the wrong entity, a statement missing for an account, and anything the country reference's *Event-driven obligations* flags. Label each one confirmed (the line and the rule are both checked, with the source), strong (the rule is clear, one fact is unconfirmed), or guess. A guess never leads the list.
6. **Decisions:** one row per decision the findings force (decision, deadline, the agent's pick and why). Price a structural question (salary against dividend, clearing a director balance) only roughly, and label it an estimate for the tax agent to confirm.
7. **Write the review** to `reviews/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>-<topic>.md` with flat frontmatter (`title`, `entity`, `created`, `sources` as a list of paths). Figures are the accountant's unless labelled an estimate.
8. **Draft the messages** in the operator's voice under the tax playbook's one-company, one-role rule: recording and classification questions to the bookkeeper first; treatment questions to the tax agent only once the books are final. Each open question the accountant must answer becomes a task (`tax`, `entity:<slug>`, `accountant`), or threads onto an existing one.
9. **Update the profile:** when the operator approves the books, set `booked_through` to the period's last month.

**Reply:** the year in three lines (sales, profit, tax provided), the top findings with their labels and cost, the decisions with picks, the review's path, and the drafted messages.
