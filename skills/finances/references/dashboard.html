    How it works

    Statements in, this page out

    Download a statement or export from your bank, card or payment platform and hand it to your agent (or drop it in projects/finances/data/inbox/). It is filed once, proven against its own balances, and its lines join this page. Categories come from RULES.md: how you categorise in plain words, and the patterns your agent learns from it and from what you approve here, so a rule you add today re-reads every month. Everything under data/ and this page stay on this machine, out of your home's history.

    Ask your agent

    • "Import ~/Downloads/october.csv"
    • "Why was October's spending high?"
    • "How much tax do I owe right now?"
    • "Close last month for the accountant"
    • "Categorise the new transactions"

    What the page never does

    • Change a transaction's currency: lines stay as the bank wrote them, and only totals convert, at each month's central-bank rate.
    • Trust a statement that doesn't add up: it's refused at import.
    • Pay, file or send anything: tasks and drafts only.
